## 2. Aggr.trade — aggr.trade
Free, no-login browser tape/order-flow viewer for crypto: live panels for Price, Volume, CVD and Liquidations, trade-size thresholds (trades >100K, liquidations >50K), a "REKTS" liquidation feed and 16 aggregated markets (e.g. BINANCE_FUTURES:BTCUSDT) **[vendor/observed]** — aggr.trade (fetched 2026-09-21; it is a JS app that exposed no pricing, about or docs link in the DOM at load). No pricing page or paid tier was found this pass, so "free" is an observation, not a published price — flagged below. An Android app "Aggr" exists, described as "a real-time crypto order-flow (tape) viewer that aggregates large trades and liquidations across top exchanges" **[vendor app listing / independent platform]** — play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.crypto.aggrs. **Segment**: crypto perp/futures traders who read the tape and liquidation feed, usually alongside a charting platform.

**Pricing**: one-time, lifetime licence for 2 PCs — **Independent $579** **[vendor]** (jigsawtrading.com/blog/jigsaw-pricing-explained; jigsawtrading.com daytradr page shows $579); **Professional $879** and **Institutional $1,979** **[independent]** (discounttrading.com/jigsaw.html; bullishbears.com review 2026; propfirmapp.com review 2026). Live *execution* from the platform needs an optional **$50/mo or $500/yr** subscription; SIM, demo and prop-evaluation trading are free forever **[vendor]** — jigsawtrading.com/blog/jigsaw-pricing-explained, corroborated **[independent]** — discounttrading.com/jigsaw.html. **Platform support**: Windows-only, 64-bit; "Mac users run it through Bootcamp or Parallels" **[independent]** — discounttrading.com/jigsaw.html. Brokers/data: Tradovate, CQG, Rithmic, NinjaTrader, StoneX, IQFeed, plus direct integrations for Interactive Brokers TWS, NinjaTrader 8 and MT5; CME, ICE, Eurex, CBOE, HKEX, JPX, ASX, B3 and NYSE/Nasdaq stocks **[vendor]** — jigsawtrading.com (pricing/product page FAQ). **Order-flow features [vendor]**: Depth & Sales DOM/price ladder with automatic order-type and exit attachment, Reconstructed Tape (re-aggregates fragmented fills into original size), Pace-of-Tape Smart Gauges, Auction Vista order-book heatmap history with large-trade circles, Depth Histogram / Flip / Price Delta / Cumulative Delta / Volume Delta charts, Journalytix trade analytics, simulator, audio news squawk (jigsawtrading.com; corroborated **[independent]** — discounttrading.com/jigsaw.html). **Segment**: retail futures day traders/scalpers and prop-firm evaluation traders; 23% of customers report trading full-time **[vendor survey]** — jigsawtrading.com FAQ.

## 6. TradingView — tradingview.com (and the Tradovate ownership correction)
**Pricing [vendor]** (tradingview.com/pricing/, annual billing): Free **$0**; Essential **$12.95/mo**; Plus **$29.95/mo**; Premium **$59.95/mo**; Ultimate **$199.95/mo** (30-day trials on the first three, 14-day on Ultimate). Volume footprint, volume profile, TPO and volume candles appear in the feature list of each paid tier from Essential upward. **Order-flow features [vendor]**: a native volume-footprint chart type showing buy/sell volume per price level with Value Area, POC, volume delta and imbalance detection plus alerts; intraday footprints request 1-tick intrabar data "for professional plans" (otherwise 1-second), and precision degrades deeper into history (tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000726164-volume-footprint-charts-a-complete-guide/). DOM: a Depth-of-Market pane in the Trade panel for accounts linked to a broker that supports Tier 2 data (tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000516459-depth-of-market-dom-what-it-is-and-how-traders-can-use-it/). **Platforms [vendor]**: web, desktop and mobile apps (tradingview.com/pricing/). **Segment**: broad retail, with the order-flow/DOM surfaces aimed at futures and crypto traders who want footprint/DOM inside a general charting subscription.

**Tradovate ownership — the brief's premise does not hold.** TradingView does not own Tradovate. The documented acquirer is NinjaTrader: "NinjaTrader Group, LLC… has acquired Tradovate Holdings, LLC… for $115 million," announced 2022-01-12 **[press release]** — businesswire.com/news/home/20220112005309/en/NinjaTrader-Acquires-Tradovate (Finance Magnates headlined it "NinjaTrader Buys Tradovate in $115 Million Deal" **[independent]** — financemagnates.com/forex/ninjatrader-buys-tradovate-in-115-million-deal/). After that acquisition, NinjaTrader itself was bought by Kraken: announced 2025-03-20 at **$1.5B** and **completed 2025-05-01**, with Kraken stating it "will continue to operate NinjaTrader as a standalone platform under the Kraken suite of trading and payments applications" **[press release]** — businesswire.com/news/home/20250501627804/en/Kraken-Completes-Acquisition-of-NinjaTrader and kraken.com/press/releases/kraken-to-acquire-ninjatrader-introducing-the-next-era-of-professional-trading. Tradovate survives as a brand and brokerage inside that group: a Tradovate login now "unlocks all NinjaTrader platforms across desktop, web, and mobile" with no new account or approvals needed **[vendor]** — ninjatrader.com/tradovate-to-ninjatrader-login/, and Tradovate remains a TradingView broker partner (Futures tradable from TradingView charts; commissions as low as $0.09/micro and $0.59/standard futures contract) **[vendor]** — tradingview.com/broker/Tradovate/. Tradovate was never absorbed by TradingView; TradingView's order-flow/DOM sits on top of a brokerage it partners with.

## 7. Defunct case: MarketDelta (2003 → Chapter 7 in 2018 → absorbed into the CQG lineage)
**What it was.** MarketDelta, founded in 2003 by Trevor Harnett, created the Footprint® chart and is the ancestor of much of today's order-flow UI: "MarketDelta initially delivered Footprint charts using a custom white label version of Linnsoft Investor R/T. From 2016, the MarketDelta offering was based upon a custom white label version of CQG Integrated Client" **[independent]** — emojitrading.com/docs/order-flow-basics/what-do-we-mean-by-order-flow/background-history/. "Footprint" remains a MarketDelta registered trademark, which is why competing vendors renamed the chart type (Sierra Chart "Numbers Bars", NinjaTrader "Volumetric Bars", MotiveWave "Volume Imprint", MultiCharts "Volume Delta") **[independent]** — same page. Brokers sold it as "MarketDelta Desktop… powered exclusively by CQG" with Footprint® charts, EZ Levels, DOM ladders and CQG data **[independent]** — gffbrokers.com/platforms/marketdelta-desktop.
**What happened, and when.** The same independent history states: "In 2018 Market Delta filed for Chapter 7 bankruptcy protection following several years of defending itself against litigation" **[independent, single source — flagged]**. Corroboration is partial: Tracxn lists MarketDelta as a "deadpooled" company founded 2003 by Trevor Harnett, based in Batavia (US) **[independent, snippet]** — tracxn.com/d/companies/marketdelta/__58UQVGGyDEAdvCpNTLz_ZDaJ0vwk6iU51VkdohzUSbo, and RocketReach shows Harnett as "2003-2018 Founder and CEO @ MarketDelta" **[independent, snippet]** — rocketreach.co/trevor-harnett-email_92905133. Domain archaeology agrees with a late-2010s death: Wayback captures of marketdelta.com return normal content through 2015-Oct 2016, switch to 301/302 redirects from Nov 2016, then error/near-empty responses from Aug 2019 through Oct 2022; the 2018-08-23 capture still carried the title "MarketDelta – The Leader in Footprint Charts, Order Flow Analysis, and Volume Profile" **[independent]** — my Wayback CDX queries, web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=marketdelta.com. Today the domain resolves (45.79.169.203) but HTTP requests fail (curl returned http_code=000 on 2026-09-21) **[independent]** — live check.
**Where users went.** In Sept 2019 broker Optimus Futures told clients "the MarketDelta platform by MarketDelta.com along with FootPrint Charts is no longer available" and routed them to Optimum Flow, Sierra Chart, Inside Edge, CQG Desktop, MotiveWave, Bookmap and Jigsaw — i.e. the migration was platform-by-platform, not to one successor **[independent]** — community.optimusfutures.com/t/marketdelta-platform-alternatives-footprint-charts-volume-profile-order-flow-analysis-software/2955. The functional migration target was the footprint chart type itself, now native everywhere under local names (Sierra Chart Numbers Bars, NinjaTrader Volumetric Bars, MotiveWave Volume Imprint, MultiCharts Volume Delta — emojitrading.com, above), and MarketDelta's desktop lineage lives on inside CQG: several broker pages still describe "MarketDelta Desktop powered by CQG" with no shutdown notice **[independent, treated as legacy/stale]** — gffbrokers.com/platforms/marketdelta-desktop, stage5trading.com/platform-solutions/platforms/marketdelta-desktop/. Adjacent ownership note: CQG itself was acquired by Broadridge — agreement announced 2026-02-06, completion reported 2026-05-01 **[press releases]** — broadridge.com/press-release/2026/broadridge-to-acquire-cqg (fetched) and broadridge.com/press-release/2026/broadridge-acquires-cqg (snippet).
