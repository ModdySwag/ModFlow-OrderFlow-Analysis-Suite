# Fact-refresh digest — NinjaTrader & MetaTrader 5

Read date 2026-09-21. Each claim carries the exact page read and a vendor-stated / independent tag. Prices and builds move monthly.

## 1. NinjaTrader (ninjatrader.com)

**Ownership (2026).** Kraken announced the $1.5B acquisition of NinjaTrader on 2025-03-20, to close H1 2025, with NinjaTrader operating as a standalone platform under Kraken (vendor-stated — https://www.kraken.com/press/releases/kraken-to-acquire-ninjatrader-introducing-the-next-era-of-professional-trading). Completion was announced as "Kraken Completes Acquisition of NinjaTrader" on 2025-05-01 (vendor-stated — https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250501627804/en/Kraken-Completes-Acquisition-of-NinjaTrader). Wikipedia records the parent as Kraken (2025–present), deal dated 2025-05-02, $1.5B, founder Raymond Deux, CEO Martin Franchi, HQ Chicago (independent — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NinjaTrader). Structure in 2026: NinjaTrader Group, LLC is the parent; NinjaTrader, LLC owns the platform; NinjaTrader Clearing, LLC d/b/a NinjaTrader, Kraken Derivatives US and Tradovate is the CFTC FCM (NFA ID 0309379) (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/pricing/). The Pulse page footer reads "© 2026 NinjaTrader Group, LLC. All rights reserved. By Payward." and "by PAYWARD © 2011–2026 Payward, Inc." (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/trading-platform/ninjatrader-pulse/). Franchi is still CEO per Traders Magazine's 2026 outlook (independent — https://www.tradersmagazine.com/news/outlook-2026-martin-franchi-ninjatrader/).

**Pricing as published today.** Free plan: $0/month, $0.39 per side Micro & e-Nano, $1.29 per side Standard. Monthly: $99/month, $0.29 Micro, $0.99 Standard. Lifetime: $1,499 one-time, $0.09 Micro, $0.59 Standard. Commissions are per side (round trip doubles them) and exchange, clearing and NFA fees are extra (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/pricing/). Intraday margins of $10–$20 (e-Nano), $50 (Micros), $500 (E-minis) and $1,000 (crude, gold, natural gas) are on the same page (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/pricing/). Independent fee check: a $35/month inactivity fee applies if you log in without placing round trades, and wire withdrawals cost $30 (independent — https://brokerchooser.com/broker-reviews/ninjatrader-review/ninjatrader-fees). NinjaTrader says BrokerChooser named it "Best Futures Broker" for 2025 and 2026 with no application fee paid (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/pricing/).

**What needs a funded live account.** "Just add funds to your live account to unlock powerful tools like real-time market data, Order Flow+, and Market Replay"; any amount above $0 qualifies (ACH/debit minimum $5) and benefits pause at a $0 balance (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/pricing/). Pulse is the exception — "Available instantly—no funding required", free for NinjaTrader and Tradovate users (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/pricing/ and https://ninjatrader.com/trading-platform/ninjatrader-pulse/). Charting, backtesting and simulated trading are free with no time limit and no funding (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/pricing/). Order Flow+ can be bought without a funded account for "$59/month as a standalone Account Add-On", and the help guide states Order Flow+ studies are included for all lifetime licence users (vendor-stated — https://support.ninjatrader.com/eu/s/article/How-Can-I-Add-NinjaTraders-Order-Flow-Features-to-My-Account and https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/order_flow_plus.htm).

**Platform support and release line.** Desktop, web and mobile clients; the desktop minimum requirement is Windows 11 or Windows Server 2016+ 64-bit with .NET 4.8 — no macOS or Linux build is listed (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/pricing/ and https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/minimum_system_requirements.htm). Current release line is 8.1: 8.1.8.0 released 2026-07-21, patch 8.1.8.1 on 2026-07-24, 8.1.8.2 on 2026-08-11; the previous minor was 8.1.7 on 2026-05-13 (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/release_notes.htm and https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/8_1_8.htm). 8.1.8 added Single Stock Futures with a dedicated workspace, a Calendar earnings tab, stock hot lists and manual lockout for prop accounts (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/8_1_8.htm). 8.1.7 made the Static SuperDOM free for all users and no longer futures-only, and sped last-price updates to 50ms (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/8_1_7.htm).

**Order-flow feature set.** The Order Flow+ suite is Order Flow Cumulative Delta, Delta Bars, Market Depth Map, Price On Volume Bars, Trade Detector, Volume Profile, Volumetric Bars (the footprint chart) and VWAP (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/order_flow_plus.htm). Volumetric bars show bid/ask volume per price and imbalance (default ratio 1.5, minimum difference 10); volume profile exposes POC and value area with Buy/Sell, Delta, Heat, Outline, Time Color and Heat Map modes (vendor-stated — https://support.ninjatrader.com/s/article/Volumetric-Bars-Order-Flow and https://support.ninjatrader.com/s/article/Volume-Profile-Order-Flow-on-NinjaTrader-Desktop). Market Profile/TPO comes from the Volume Profile indicator's letter mode (vendor-stated — https://prop.ninjatrader.com/blogs/order-flow-trading-futures-prop-traders/). DOM is the SuperDOM in Static and Dynamic variants; Chart Trader is a panel on the right edge of any chart (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/superdom.htm and https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/chart_trader.htm). Market Replay runs through the built-in Playback connection, with Market Replay or Historical modes, a DVD-style slider, speed control and a Replay101 account whose history resets on disconnect (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/playback.htm).

**Layout, hotkeys and order-entry safety.** The Control Center is the always-open shell holding Orders, Strategies, Executions, Positions, Accounts, Log and Messages tabs (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/control_center.htm). There are six order-entry windows — Basic Entry, Chart Trader, FX Pro, FX Board, Order Ticket and SuperDOM — all requiring a three-button mouse (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/order_entry.htm). Workspaces carry version history: "By default 10 previously saved versions of your workspaces will be retained", restorable via Tools > Database Management and configurable in Options, with import/export from the Workspaces menu and SHIFT+F3 to cycle open workspaces (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/workspaces_menu.htm). Window linking is colour-based across charts, order entry, alerts, Time & Sales, news, FX Board and Market Analyzer, with chart interval linking and a "Link All" option (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/linking_windows.htm). Hot Keys live in the Hot Keys window (Control Center > Tools), are window-sensitive, split into Global and Order Entry categories, warn on conflicts and can be printed (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/working_with_hot_keys.htm). Safety grammar: "Confirm order placement" opens a popup for each order "preventing an accidental order submission"; "Use order entry hot keys" must be enabled for hot-key entry; "Last order button keyboard focus" keeps a button hot; Auto close position flattens chosen instruments at a set time; order states are colour-coded (Initialized yellow, Submitted/Change submitted/Cancel pending orange, Accepted/Working/Suspended in order-type colour) (vendor-stated — https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/options_trading.htm and https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpguides/nt8/order_state_definitions.htm).

**Independent criticism and praise.** Praise: an add-on ecosystem spanning 150+ countries, Nano contracts for fine sizing, CME Single Stock Futures launched July 2026, and "The market replay function is a standout feature" (independent — https://www.daytrading.com/ninjatrader). Criticism: fined $250k in 2025 for an inadequate AML program and supervisory failures; concern that "traders who value long-term stability may have reservations" under Kraken ownership; intraday margins "can spike by as much as four times" in the 15 minutes before major releases (independent — https://www.daytrading.com/ninjatrader). Trustpilot shows 3.8/5 from 1,226 reviews on a claimed profile, with recurring praise for named support staff and platform criticism such as "Ninja is a good platform for futures, all they need to do is give it a facelift, make it more intuitive, trader friendly" (independent — https://www.trustpilot.com/review/ninjatrader.com). Off-hours support coverage is flagged as thin (independent — https://www.daytrading.com/ninjatrader).

## 2. MetaTrader 5 (metatrader5.com)

**Pricing and licensing as published today.** MT5 is free to traders — the download page offers the full feature set "for free right now"; there is no subscription, no funded-account gate and no MetaQuotes-set commission (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/download). MetaQuotes is a pure vendor: "MetaQuotes is a software development company and does not provide investment or brokerage services" (site footer) (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/company). Revenue comes from brokers under three licence options — Entry (1,000 real accounts), Standard (25,000) and Enterprise (200,000) — with no published price; buyers request access rather than buy online (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/brokers/buy). Not offered as SaaS: "We do not offer SaaS or other similar solutions" (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/brokers/buy). Owner is MetaQuotes Ltd, established 2000, Cyprus-based B2B developer with worldwide offices; footers read © 2000–2026 MetaQuotes Ltd (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/company). No 2024–2026 acquisition or control change was found; 2026 company news is product and event material, e.g. "MetaTrader 5 Named Best Multi-Asset Trading Platform" on 2026-02-25 (vendor-stated — https://www.metaquotes.net/en/company/news/5520). Separate paid extras exist around the platform: MQL5 Market apps and Virtual Hosting (VPS) (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help).

**Platform support and current version.** Clients are Windows, macOS, Linux, WebTerminal, Android and iPhone/iPad; the macOS and Linux desktop installs run "using a third-party software designed for Microsoft Windows to run on Unix-like operating systems" (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/download and https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/startworking/start_advanced). The web terminal is OS-agnostic (macOS, Linux, Windows; Chrome, Safari, Edge, Opera, Firefox) with 30 indicators and 24 graphical objects (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/trading-platform/web-trading). Current build is 6180 dated 2026-09-03, the newest entry on the release-notes page as read today; recent builds above it are 6140 (2026-08-20), 6090 (2026-07-30), 6060 (2026-07-23), 5830 (2026-04-24) and 5800 (2026-04-16) (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes). 2026 feature direction is AI, not order flow: builds 6060–6180 add a built-in AI Assistant with MCP tools that can add indicators and read tester reports (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes/terminal/2447 and https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes/terminal/2464).

**2025–2026 UI redesign announcements.** Build 5800 (2026-04-16) is sub-titled "the first stage of enhancements to the main trading dialog": "We have started a comprehensive redesign of the trading dialog to make it more intuitive and functional", adding a built-in Depth of Market in the dialog and a side panel for order-type switching and one-click trading toggles; "Upcoming versions will provide further enhancements for stop level placement and risk management" (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes/terminal/2436). Build 5430 (2025-11-13): "The legacy GDI framework has been replaced with the modern Blend2D engine" for chart rendering (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes). Build 5100 (2025) replaced Subversion with Git in MQL5 Storage and redesigned the MetaEditor Navigator; build 5800 also moved built-in help off "the legacy CHM format" onto a web engine (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes and https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes/terminal/2436).

**Admitted UX debt.** MetaQuotes' own stated debt is technical and incremental — "legacy CHM" help, "legacy GDI" charting, Subversion replaced by Git, and a trading dialog described as a "first stage" redesign. No statement of broader interface debt was found; the redesign notes are the admission (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes/terminal/2436).

**How order flow is done on MT5.** Natively, only via Depth of Market. On exchange-mode instruments it shows "real prices and order volumes from market participants"; on OTC instruments it may be formed from broker quotes and, if the broker provides no volumes, "the DOM window functions as a scalping tool". Levels shown are set by the broker and "availability of the Depth of Market feature for exchange instruments is not guaranteed and depends on your broker" (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/trading/depth_of_market). No footprint, volume profile, cumulative delta or market profile appears anywhere in the MT5 help table of contents — a checked absence, not a disproof (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help). Order flow is therefore third-party: paid utilities such as "Order flow footprint chart" and the free "Order Flow Standart" on MQL5 Market, plus vendor builds such as ClusterDelta's "#Footprint – Orderflow chart" for MetaTrader 4/5 (independent — https://www.mql5.com/en/market/product/160805, https://www.mql5.com/en/market/product/161931 and https://clusterdelta.com/footprint). An analyst page states plainly: "MetaTrader 5 does not have a native Volume Profile indicator, but several third-party options are compatible" (independent — https://fazencapital.com/learn/en/volume-profile-trading-poc-value-area). A comparison table rates MT5 order-flow tools "Limited / not available" against NinjaTrader's "Via add-ons (extra cost)" (independent — https://thepropfirmguide.com/quantower/). Traders still ask in forums whether "there are any decent Order flow indicators for MT5 available yet" (independent — https://nexusfi.com/showthread.php?t=43851&page=2).

**Tips, tooltips and onboarding.** No "Tip of the Day" or lessons system is documented on metatrader5.com: it appears in neither the MT5 help table of contents nor the 2025–2026 release notes, so this is reported as a checked absence (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help and https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes). What is documented is contextual tooltips — hovering deal icons on a chart shows "ticket, deal type, volume, symbol, open price and current price", and a duplication bug in the Market Watch instrument-search tooltip was fixed on 2026-08-20 (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/startworking/settings and https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes/terminal/2464). Real onboarding surfaces are demo accounts, the rebuilt web-based Help (in-panel or detached) and the AI Assistant (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/startworking and https://www.metatrader5.com/en/releasenotes/terminal/2436).

**Layout model and hotkeys.** MT5 documents no user-facing control-centre shell, workspace versioning or workspace import/export; the model is individual windows plus tools, templates and profiles (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help). Hotkeys: "Keyboard shortcuts can be assigned for calling any element of the Navigator window, except for elements of the Accounts group", set via "Set hotkey", and assigned hotkeys override predefined ones — there is no categorised Hot Keys window (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/start_advanced/hotkeys). No cross-window colour-linking for instrument sync is documented (vendor-stated — https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help).

**Independent criticism and praise.** Trustpilot: metatrader5.com sits at 1.4/5 from 206 reviews on an unclaimed profile as read today. Quotes: "Platform incredibly outdated!!" and "The MT5 platform feels incredibly outdated in today's trading world"; "Incredible how MetaTrader 5 is nearly as clumsy and frustrating to use as its predecessor"; "The charts in MetaTrader 5 look like they were drawn by hand"; "MT5 is freezing from time to time"; "Every time there's a platform update, MT5 starts crashing or freezing in the middle of sessions"; one reviewer rates v5.0.5370 as better for scalping than recent builds (independent — https://www.trustpilot.com/review/metatrader5.com). Praise: Capterra reviewers call MT5 reliable, easy, "requires little data to operate" and "absolutely free", with the mobile app the most common complaint (independent — https://www.capterra.com/p/230413/MetaTrader-5/reviews/). Independent comparison: MT5 dominates the retail forex segment and brokers report offering it "alongside limited access to MetaTrader" products of their own (independent — https://www.forexbrokers.com/guides/metatrader-5-brokers).
